Copilot now uses a bounded collector: it retains a 16 KiB byte tail, copies the retained tail rather than keeping oversized backing buffers, and allows at most one second for stderr to drain after the process exit is observed. The collector stops data/end/close listeners and clears its retained bytes on disposal; a small error guard remains on the owned stream so late transport errors cannot crash the daemon. This bounds the collector, not every upstream transport buffer.
